THE LEGEND ENDS

A Story With More Than One Side

Athena's golden shield bearing the image of Medusa

What did you learn?

  • 📖
    Medusa was a famous figure in Greek mythology who was once a peaceful young woman.
  • 🐍
    She was transformed into a Gorgon with living snakes for hair after a terrible curse.
  • 🛡️
    Perseus defeated her safely by using the reflection in his polished bronze shield.
“The legend of Medusa reminds us that every story can have more than one side.”
